How a start works

The flag

There are no lights and no countdown. There is one person with one flag, and six rules everybody keeps.

  1. Roll to the line

    Both cars stop with front wheels on the white line. The marshal checks the gap.

    Watch this moment →

  2. She asks twice

    The starter looks at each driver in turn. A nod means ready. No nod, no start.

    Watch this moment →

  3. Flag up

    When the flag is above her head, hands stay on the wheel and cars stay still.

    Watch this moment →

  4. Flag down

    The run starts when the flag passes her shoulder. Moving earlier loses the run.

    Watch this moment →

  5. Hold your lane

    Left car stays left to the corner. Crossing the centre line loses the run.

    Watch this moment →

  6. No second starts

    One run per pair. The result stands, whatever happened.
